About Us

Vasavi College of Engineering is a reputed engineering institution located in Ibrahimbagh, Hyderabad. The college focuses on quality technical education, innovation, research and holistic development.

The institution provides students with opportunities to develop technical knowledge, communication skills, leadership abilities and problem-solving skills.
